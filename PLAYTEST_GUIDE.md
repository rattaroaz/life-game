# LifeSim Playtest Guide

## Quick Start (5-Minute Fun Path)

### 1. Launch & Create Your Sim
```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

- Click **Create-A-Sim**
- Customize your Sim (appearance, traits, aspiration)
- Name your household
- Click **Move in**

### 2. Learn the Controls

**Game Speeds:**
- `Space` - Pause/Unpause
- `1` / `2` / `3` - Speed settings
- `W` `A` `S` `D` - Pan camera
- Mouse wheel - Zoom

**Modes (top center):**
- **LIVE** - Control Sims and play
- **BUILD** - Place walls, doors, windows
- **BUY** - Purchase furniture and objects

### 3. Satisfy Needs (First 5 Minutes)

Watch the **Needs panel** (left sidebar) - keep all 6 needs above 30:

1. **Hunger** 🍕
   - Click Fridge → "Grab snack" (quick) or "Get ingredients" → Cook → Eat (better)
   
2. **Energy** 💤
   - Click Bed → "Sleep" (double bed has 2 slots)
   - Or sit on Sofa → "Sit & relax"
   
3. **Bladder** 🚽
   - Click Toilet → "Use toilet"
   
4. **Hygiene** 🚿
   - Click Shower → "Shower"
   - Or Sink → "Wash hands" (light refresh)
   
5. **Fun** 🎮
   - TV → "Watch TV"
   - Computer → "Use computer"
   - Arcade, Chess, Guitar, Paint, Exercise Mat, etc.
   
6. **Social** 💬
   - Click **People panel** (left sidebar)
   - Click someone → **Talk** button
   - Or double-click them in the world
   - Try: Talk, Joke, Compliment, Deep conversation

### 4. Explore Social Interactions

- **People panel** shows neighbors and NPCs
- Travel to different places using the **City map** (left sidebar)
- Visit the Cafe, Park, Plaza to meet more people
- Build relationships:
  - Friendship 50+ → "Good friends" 💙
  - Friendship 80+ → "Best friends" 💖
  - Romance 50+ → "Getting romantic" 💕

### 5. Build Skills & Career

**Skills:**
- Cooking (fridge/stove)
- Logic (computer, bookshelf, chess)
- Charisma (computer, talking)
- Fitness (exercise mat, punching bag)
- Creativity (easel, guitar, painting)

Watch for **level-up notifications** with ⭐!

**Career:**
- Left sidebar → **Career** section
- Join **Office Worker** or **Chef**
- Work schedule appears automatically
- Gain promotions by building skills and performance

### 6. Build & Buy Mode

**Buy Mode:**
- Select an object from the catalog
- Click the lot to place it
- Costs deducted from household funds

**Build Mode:**
- Place walls, doors, windows
- Click tiles to add/remove
- "Erase" tool removes walls

### 7. Watch Autonomy AI

When you're NOT giving commands:
- Sims will care for themselves
- They'll eat when hungry, sleep when tired
- They'll practice skills for their career
- They'll socialize with nearby Sims

**Visual Feedback:**
- **Thought Bubble** (top right) - what they're thinking
- **Moodlets** (top center) - active mood modifiers
- **Action Queue** (bottom center) - current and upcoming actions
- **Activity Window** (top center) - what they're doing now

### 8. Save & Load

- Click **Save** (top right) anytime
- Main Menu → **Load Game** to continue later
- Auto-saves to browser local storage

All 6 needs have multiple recovery paths:

| Need | Objects | Interactions |
|------|---------|-------------|
| Hunger | Fridge, Stove, Dining Table | Snack, Cook, Eat |

## Expected Playthrough

**First 30 Minutes:**
1. Create Sim and move in (2 min)
2. Explore the lot, learn controls (3 min)
3. Satisfy immediate needs (5 min)
4. Meet a neighbor, start conversation (5 min)
5. Join a career (1 min)
6. Practice a skill (5 min)
7. Build/buy something (3 min)
8. Go to work, return with pay (5 min)
9. Save game (1 min)

**Goal:** Feel like you're playing a fun, interactive life sim with real progression and emergent stories!

## Known Features

- **Moodlets** show temporary mood boosts (well fed, pumped up, etc.)
- **Thought bubbles** show what Sims are thinking
- **Skill notifications** celebrate level-ups
- **Relationship milestones** announce friendship/romance progress
- **Action queue** shows what's coming next
- **Autonomy AI** keeps Sims alive when you're not directing them
- **Day/night cycle** with weather
- **City map** with 10+ places to visit
- **10 NPCs** with unique personalities and bios

## Troubleshooting

- **Sim won't move:** Check if they're stuck - cancel action and try again
- **Can't interact:** Switch to LIVE mode
- **Needs dropping fast:** Some traits make needs decay faster (Foodie → hunger, Lazy → energy)
- **No one to talk to:** Travel to Cafe, Park, or visit neighbors' houses
- **Out of money:** Go to work! Or sell unwanted objects (not implemented yet - buy carefully)
